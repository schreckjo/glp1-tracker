Injection site effectiveness weekly

Each site is judged on the three things that matter: are you losing weight, are symptoms low, is your energy good.
How this works. For each site, average the weight change, symptom burden, and energy across the days that followed each injection. Each value is compared against your own overall average. Two or three signals better than your average → Working well. One signal better → Mixed. None better → Lagging. Sites need at least 2 injections before a verdict is shown.

Caveat: a good week makes whatever site you used look good. Trust the ranking after 4–6 weeks of rotating between sites.
